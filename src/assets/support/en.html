OneVibe Support

If you need help, have questions, or want to report an issue, we are here to assist you.

Contact Us

We usually respond within 24–48 hours.

Common Support Topics

  • Account Issues – login problems, account recovery, profile settings
  • Payment Issues – billing, subscriptions, refunds
  • Bug Reports – app crashes, unexpected behavior
  • Feature Requests – suggestions for new features or improvements
  • Privacy Concerns – data handling, account deletion, blocking
  • Content Issues – reporting inappropriate content or accounts

When Contacting Us, Please Include

  • Your device model (e.g. iPhone 15, Samsung Galaxy S24)
  • Operating system version (e.g. iOS 17.4, Android 14)
  • App version (found in Settings → About)
  • A clear description of the issue
  • Screenshots or screen recordings if applicable

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?

On the login screen, tap "Forgot Password" and follow the instructions sent to your registered email or phone number.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account inside the app. Alternatively, email us at support@onevibe.ai and we will process your request within 7 business days.

How do I report a user or content?

Tap the three-dot menu (⋯) on any post or profile and select "Report". Our team reviews all reports within 48 hours.

I am having trouble with payments. What should I do?

Email us at support@onevibe.ai with your transaction details and we will investigate and resolve the issue promptly.

How do I request or delete my personal data?

Contact us at support@onevibe.ai with the subject line "Data Request" and we will respond within 7 business days.
